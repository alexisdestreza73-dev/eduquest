⚡ How to Level Up
Your total XP = Base XP (from GPA) + Bonus XP (from quizzes). The higher your combined XP, the higher your level.
🎓
Improve Your GPA
Your GPA converts directly into base XP. A 1.25 GPA = 950 XP. A 2.0 GPA = 800 XP. This is your starting point.
🎮
Complete Quizzes
Each quiz adds bonus XP on top of your base. CP = +50, HCI = +55, FIS = +60. Score higher = earn more bonus XP.
🏅
Earn Badges
Unlock 10 badges by hitting milestones — first quiz, perfect score, reaching Level 5+, and more.
👑
Reach Max Level
Hit 1000+ XP to reach Level 10: Supreme — the highest rank in EduQuest.
⚔️ Tied on GPA? Quizzes Break the Tie!
If two students have the same GPA, they start with the same base XP and show as TIE on the leaderboard. But once they play quizzes, bonus XP separates them — whoever scores higher on the quizzes earns more XP and climbs ahead. This means even a student with a lower GPA can overtake a rival by acing the quizzes!
📊 Quiz Scores & Live Dashboard Updates
After each question, you instantly see whether your answer was correct ✓ or wrong ✗ with a full explanation. When the quiz ends, your final score, percentage, and XP earned are shown on screen. Your dashboard updates automatically — XP bar, level, badges, and leaderboard rank all refresh the moment you finish. No need to reload — everything is live.
